Weather Triggers

Every race weekend on the calendar is scored against a small set of thresholds. This page is where you decide what those thresholds should be — by watching the tire and engine call each one produces, before it goes anywhere near the crew's phones.

Trigger Sandbox

Pick a scenario, move a threshold, and read the tire and engine call it produces. This runs the shipped classifier — the same classifyRaceDayRisk the schedule board uses, mirrored by classify_race_day_risk in the refresh script — so nothing here is a simulation of the logic. It is the logic.

What reaches the crew

StateWhere it showsWho hears about it
Weather risk high Schedule board, weather column Slack or Discord webhook, but only for a real forecast inside 7 days of the weekend
Watch conditions Schedule board, weather column Nobody — visible on the dashboard only
Good track window Schedule board, weather column Nobody

Climate normals never page anyone. A ten-year average is not news, and paging on one would train the crew to ignore the channel. Alerts are also deduplicated by digest, so an unchanged picture stays quiet on the second and third day.
